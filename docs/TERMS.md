## 2. What GameMate is

GameMate is a free desktop app for Windows that helps you while you play video games. It answers questions with an AI model that runs on your PC or, if you choose, with Google Gemini. It can look at your screen when you ask, read game wikis, find the games installed on your PC, listen to your voice and read answers aloud. What each feature does with your data is explained in the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).

GameMate is free of charge. There is no account, no subscription and no in-app purchase.
