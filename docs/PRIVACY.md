# Privacy Policy

Version 2 · Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to your personal data when you use GameMate. It is written to meet the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act (TDDDG) and the Personal Data Protection Law of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (PDPL).

## 1. Who is responsible

**Omar Abulebda**, the provider of GameMate.
Email: [TSCKey1@outlook.com](mailto:TSCKey1@outlook.com)

## 2. The short version

- GameMate runs on your PC. **We do not receive, collect or store any of your data.** There is no GameMate server, no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
- Everything GameMate keeps stays on your PC, in your Windows user folder.
- GameMate only connects to other companies' services when you use a feature that needs them. Features that send your data elsewhere (Google Gemini, web search, online lookups, finding your games' wikis) are **off until you turn them on**, and you can turn them off at any time. You can turn on finding your games and online lookups on the screen GameMate shows after you accept the Terms of Use, or any of these features later in **Settings**.
- **Delete my data** in **Settings → Privacy & legal** removes everything GameMate stored on your PC.

## 3. What GameMate keeps on your PC

GameMate stores the following in its folder in your Windows user profile, readable only by your Windows account. Storing it is necessary to provide the features you use (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG).

| What                                                                                                                | Why                                                          | How long                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Your settings, including when you accepted these documents and gave each consent                                    | To remember your choices, and as the record of your consents | Until you change them or delete your data                             |
| Your Gemini API key, if you add one, encrypted by Windows                                                           | To use Gemini                                                | Until you remove it, withdraw your Gemini consent or delete your data |
| AI models, GameMate's AI engine, speech models and voices you downloaded                                            | To run the AI on your PC                                     | Until you remove them or delete your data                             |
| Games found or added: name, folder or program, wiki address                                                         | To help with the game you are playing                        | Until you withdraw that consent or delete your data                   |
| A log of what GameMate did (for example starting the AI or checking for updates), without your questions or answers | To find and fix problems                                     | About 1 MB, older entries are overwritten; deleted with your data     |

Your conversations, screenshots, voice recordings and what the companion noticed are **kept in memory only** and are gone when you close the chat, switch the companion off or quit GameMate. Screenshots and voice recordings are never saved to disk.

## 4. What GameMate does on your PC

- **Your screen** is captured only when you send a question with a screenshot, press the in-game shortcut with screenshots turned on, or while the companion is set to watch your game. You choose this in the app; the companion is off by default.
- **Your microphone** is used only while voice input is on and you press the voice shortcut or 🎤. Your speech is turned into text on your PC.
- **The AI model** that runs on your PC (GameMate's own engine, or Ollama if you use it) receives your questions and screenshots on your PC only.

## 5. Optional features that need your consent

These features are off until you turn them on. Turning one on is your consent to what is described here (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR, § 25 (1) TDDDG, PDPL). You can withdraw each consent at any time by turning the feature off; this stops the feature and does not affect anything done before. You can also turn on finding your games and online lookups on the screen GameMate shows after you accept the Terms of Use.

### Finding your games (Settings → Games)

GameMate reads the game lists of Steam, Epic Games, Battle.net and Riot on your PC, your CurseForge modpack folders and the name of the program in front. This stays on your PC. To find each game's wiki, **only the game's name** is sent to wiki.gg and Fandom. Withdrawing this consent also deletes the list of games GameMate found.

### Online lookups (Settings → AI & online)

When a question is about something in a game, GameMate sends **the topic** (for example "Ender Dragon") to that game's wiki (such as minecraft.wiki, a Fandom wiki or a wiki.gg wiki), and for Minecraft mods the mod's name to Modrinth, and downloads the page text and pictures it shows you. Your question itself is not sent.

### Web search (Settings → AI & online)

Only available while Gemini is on. For questions about what is current (the meta, patches, seasons) or what players recommend, and when the wiki has no answer, GameMate or the AI makes a short **search query** from the key words of your question and the game's name (for example "Overwatch characters currently meta"), and GameMate sends only that query to Google through the Gemini API with your API key. Your conversation, screenshots and other questions are not sent with it. Google searches the web (including forums such as Reddit) and returns a summary, the pages it used and its search suggestions, which GameMate shows with the answer. Google processes the query under the Gemini API terms and its privacy policy (see Google Gemini below). Searches count toward your API key's free Google Search allowance; if billing is turned on for your key, Google may charge for searches beyond it.

### Google Gemini (Settings → AI & online)

If you choose Gemini for answers or for the companion, GameMate sends to Google: your questions, the conversation so far, the screenshots you attach or the companion takes, what the companion noticed recently, and your API key. Google processes this under the [Gemini API Additional Terms of Service](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), as its own controller. Depending on your Google account, Google may use this data to improve its services. Google requires you to be 18 or older.

## 6. Connections that are not optional consents

- **Downloads you start** (AI models, speech models and the natural voice from Hugging Face, GameMate's AI engine from GitHub) connect to those services when you click **Download**, because you asked for them (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR). Ticking a download on that screen and pressing **Continue** also starts it.
- **Update checks** ask GitHub for the latest version of GameMate, shortly after GameMate starts and every few hours, and download a new version if there is one. We do this to deliver security fixes and improvements (our legitimate interest, Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). You can turn this off in **Settings → General**. GameMate makes no update check before you have accepted the Terms of Use.

Like any connection on the internet, these requests show the service your IP address and basic technical information (such as the app's name and version). We never receive this information.

## 7. Who receives data

Only the services you use through GameMate, each as its own controller under its own privacy policy: Google (Gemini), the game wikis you look things up on (for example Fandom, wiki.gg, minecraft.wiki), Modrinth, Hugging Face and GitHub (which belongs to Microsoft). We do not sell or share your data with anyone, because we never have it.

## 8. Transfers to other countries

Some of these services are based in, or process data in, the United States or other countries outside the EU and outside Saudi Arabia. Data reaches them only when you use the feature that connects to them. For transfers from the EU, these companies rely on safeguards under the GDPR such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. If you live in Saudi Arabia, using these features means your data is transferred outside the Kingdom at your request.

## 9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data (Art. 15), to correct it (Art. 16), to have it deleted (Art. 17), to restrict its processing (Art. 18), to receive it in a portable format (Art. 20), to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21), and to withdraw any consent at any time (Art. 7 (3)). Under the PDPL you have the right to be informed, to access your data and obtain a copy, to have it corrected, completed or updated, to have it destroyed, and to withdraw your consent.

Because everything GameMate stores is on your own PC, you can use most of these rights directly in GameMate: see and change your choices in Settings, turn optional features off, and use **Delete my data**. For data held by Google, the wikis, Hugging Face or GitHub, contact those services. For anything else, email us at [TSCKey1@outlook.com](mailto:TSCKey1@outlook.com).

## 10. Complaints

You can complain to a data protection authority, in particular the one where you live or work:

- **In the EU:** your national data protection authority ([list of EU authorities](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en)). In Germany, this is the data protection authority of your federal state.
- **In Saudi Arabia:** the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA), which supervises the PDPL ([sdaia.gov.sa](https://sdaia.gov.sa)).

## 11. Children

GameMate is meant for people aged 16 and older, or younger with the permission of a parent or legal guardian. Gemini is only for people aged 18 and older, as Google requires.

## 12. No automated decisions

GameMate makes no decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects. The AI only answers your questions.

## 13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the new version comes with a GameMate update, and GameMate shows it to you before you continue. You can always read the current version in **Settings → Privacy & legal**.
