## Languages

- GameMate now speaks **eight languages**: English, German, French, Arabic, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Italian and, new, **Hindi**.
- Turkish, Russian, Japanese, Chinese and Korean are no longer offered. If you used one of them, GameMate switches to English; you can pick another language in **⚙ Settings → General**.

## Voices

- **Kokoro is back** as the natural voice for English, Spanish, French, Italian and Portuguese, with **37 voices** to choose from, including American and British English. In **⚙ Settings → Voice**, pick one and press **Listen** to hear it.
- **Supertonic** speaks all eight languages, and is the voice for German, Arabic and Hindi.
- Windows voices are gone. When the voice you chose can't speak your language, nothing is read aloud, and the voice settings tell you why.
- Voices you already downloaded are kept. A later update that adds voices only downloads the new ones.

## Also new

- A **What’s new** window: after each update, GameMate shows what changed. You can open it again from **⚙ Settings → General → What’s new**.
- The Terms of Use and Privacy Policy now describe both voices, and are available in Hindi. You're asked to accept the updated Terms once.
- **⚙ Settings → Privacy & legal** opens the licenses of the software GameMate includes.
